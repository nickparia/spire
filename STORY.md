# Spire — the story

The bible for everything the game says. Spare, second person, past tense when it looks back. The game never explains more than this; the rest is in the sky.

## The premise

The skies did not go out all at once. They went out the way lamps do, one by one, over a long time, and the world moved on under them.

You are the last builder of the Spire: the tower that stood at the centre of all the worlds, that held the skies up, that nobody remembers raising. You do not rebuild it from memory. You rebuild it from rhythm — the groove in the stone, the drop, the set. Each sky you reach, you light.

The Dark below is not evil. It is what is left when light is forgotten. It eats what falls.

## The title

> Something put the skies out. Stack the Spire high enough to light them again, one sky at a time.

## World one — Hearth

_The column. Honest gravity, the Dark below._ This is where the Spire was cut and raised. The stone still knows the shape.

| Tidewater   | The sea remembers the Spire's shadow. It walks when you walk.                         |
| Pulse City  | They built clocks here, to keep time with the tower. The clocks are all that is left. |
| Red Canyon  | Something burst through here on its way down. Mind the fuses.                         |
| Gale Ridge  | Up here the wind has nothing to hold on to but you.                                   |
| The Glacier | Slow. The cold is honest; it only takes what you give it time to take.                |
| The Eclipse | The last sky they lit was the first to go dark. Watch for the flare.                  |
| Apex        | The top. It has been waiting, and so has what lives beneath it.                       |

**Boss: the Escape.** At Apex's summit the sky is lit, and the light wakes what put the skies out: not the Dark below, but the source above them, a vast thing of eyes and reaching tentacles. It comes down for the light. The light drops into the top slab and you run down the tower you built, breaking it floor by floor; every perfect from the climb is stored light that drives the source back. Reach the ground and the light escapes into the world.

**Revelation:** _It was never the Dark below. What put the skies out was waiting above them._

The Dark below "eats what falls"; its source above only shows itself when you take a sky back. Every world's boss is an inversion of that world's climb.

## Worlds to come

Each world is a place the Spire once stood through, with its own voice.

- **The Descent** — the roots. The voice goes quieter, more intimate; it is talking about what was buried. _Down here the stone is older than the tower. It was never asked._ What climbs up is what the first builders left in the dark long enough to want the light back. You build down. **Revelation:** _They stopped. That is all. The builders stopped._
- **The Wheel** — the mill. Mechanical, rhythmic, almost liturgical. _It turned to keep the tower fed. It turns still, for nothing._ **Boss: the Siege** — the night the millwrights stood a ring around the last lamp. **Revelation:** _The Dark is only the forgetting._
- **The Low Moon** — the tender world; it grieves rather than threatens. _Here the falling is slow. You get to watch what you let go._ **Revelation:** _The tower was never finished._
- **The last world** — **Revelation:** _You were not the first to try. You are the first to be remembered._

## The shape of the whole (Nick, Oct 7)

The worlds are the planets of one system, each with its Spire and its sky, and each with its own source above it — one of a kind, not one creature. Every boss is a different one of them. At the centre of the system is a black hole: the mega boss sits there, and it is where they came through. Beating it opens the way into their dimension: the next arc of the game is played on their side.

- Prologue: the skies went out one by one.
- I · Hearth: what the foundry remembered; "it was never the Dark below."
- II · The Descent: what was buried. The builders followed the roots down and found a door no one built; through it, something showed them the whole system (many worlds, a dark one above each, all turning round a black centre). They stopped, and lay down. The climbers were the ones who stopped waiting. "They stopped. That is all. The builders stopped."
- Later chapters reveal, world by world: there are many of them; they came from the centre; the builders stopped because they understood; the black hole is a door.
- Chapters are four paragraphs, each over its own living painting (`src/game/story.ts`, art in `public/art/story/`).

## Rules for the voice

- Never more than two sentences at a time.
- Name things plainly: the Dark, the Spire, the light. No proper nouns for the player.
- The game gives one line of exposition per boss and nothing else; the star map is the only map, because the sky is the record.
